    Euroleague Basketball — Live Venue-to-Cloud Stats Ingestion

    Remote (Europe) | 2026 | Proof of Concept & Solution Design

    I designed and built a serverless GCP pipeline that ingests live play-by-play directly from the venue, running in parallel with a legacy on-prem SQL Server system and reimplementing its stored-procedure statistics logic in the cloud.
